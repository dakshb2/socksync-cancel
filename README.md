# SockSync+: Cancel Your Socks

Keytos Hack, Mini-Challenge #1: Worst Cancel Screen Ever.

SockSync+ is a fictional socks-as-a-service subscription with a deliberately hostile cancel flow. Cancelling is possible, just miserable.

**Play it:** open `index.html` in any browser (or the GitHub Pages link, if enabled).

## The gauntlet
1. **Home page:** a pre-filled cart blocks cancelling, and emptying it adds "Empty Cart Protection". Pop-up upsells have tiny close buttons, and the big red Cancel button only cancels your thoughts of leaving.
2. **Terms of Sock:** clause 7.4 says "Leave" keeps your membership and "Stay" continues cancelling.
3. **Retention Maze:** steer a trapped cursor with W A S D to NO while YES buttons roam into your path.
4. **Are you sure you don't want to not cancel?** Swapping buttons and a pre-checked upgrade.
5. **Guilt trip:** six named socks cry as you approach "Abandon".
6. **Survey:** a Positivity Filter rewrites complaints into compliments.
7. **Offer:** a timer that never expires; hold to decline.
8. **Cancellation Department:** open 3 seconds at a time.
9. **Step 7 of 6:** fake progress, then you're finally cancelled, with stats and a list of every dark pattern you survived.

## Why
These tricks are real, and regulators fine companies for them. Building the worst one is the fastest way to learn to spot them, and to never ship one.

## Built with
Single-file HTML, CSS and JavaScript, built with Claude. No real accounts, payments or personal data.
